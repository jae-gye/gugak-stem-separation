## The finding: the excursion is NOT a numerics problem

`BF16_ADOPTION.md` set an explicit watch-item — *"if 가야금 or 양금 cliffs again under
bf16, that confirms the mechanism is not numerical."*

**It fired.** At epoch 46 the average fell from 2.05 to **0.74** in one epoch.

| epoch | 가야금 | 양금 | every other class | avg |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 45 | −1.9 | **−9.7** | unchanged | +2.05 |
| **46** | −1.8 | **−20.4** | moved < 0.5 dB | **+0.74** |
| 47 | −1.4 | −18.4 | unchanged | +1.03 |
| 55 | +0.8 | −16.3 | unchanged | +1.62 |

**양금 alone**, dropping 10.7 dB in a single epoch while all eight other classes moved by
less than half a dB. It then self-healed only partially, and is still 7 dB below its
pre-excursion level fourteen epochs later. ReduceLROnPlateau halved the LR to 1.25e-5 at
epoch 49, which stabilised but did not restore it.

This is now a **reproducible failure mode across three different numeric regimes**:

| regime | excursion | classes hit | non-finite steps |
|---|---|---|---|
| fp16 | catastrophic (−8.9, then −14.2) | 가야금 · 대금 · 양금 · 아쟁 | 5–8 per 10k |
| fp32 | mild (+0.04 → −1.36), self-healed | 가야금 · 양금 | 0 |
| **bf16** | **moderate (2.05 → 0.74), partial heal** | **양금** | **0** |

Precision changed the *severity* and it changed which classes were dragged along, but it
never removed the phenomenon. With overflow structurally impossible under bf16 and zero
non-finite steps logged, the cause has to lie in the **objective or the class scheme**,
not the arithmetic.

**Reading:** 양금 is the rarest modelled class — 9 of 91 val songs, ~6.6 h of source
material, the smallest pool by a wide margin. Under a plain L1 objective, a head with
little data and low energy is cheapest to abandon, and the earlier demo renders showed
exactly that signature: failing classes are *suppressed to near-silence*, not confused
with other instruments. Its predicted RMS sits at the same level the model outputs for
genuinely absent classes.

→ **This is an exp002 design question, not more epochs.** Candidates: loss weighting for
quiet/rare classes, per-class loss normalisation, or clumping 양금 into a broader pitched
class. Running exp001.2 longer will not fix it.

**A genuine win alongside it:** 가야금 finally went positive (−5.9 → +0.8 over the run,
improving on **68 of 68** val songs). The plucked-pair confusion that motivated the
clumping hypothesis measurably improved on its own.
